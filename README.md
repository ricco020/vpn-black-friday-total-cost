# Black Friday VPN deals 2026: total cost over 24, 27 or 36 months

Static page that ranks the Black Friday 2026 VPN plans by the total paid over a chosen period, from prices read on the providers' order pages in November 2026 (source table: https://www.vpnsmith.com/en/blog/black-friday-vpn-deals-2026). Renewal prices that are not published are flagged, not estimated.

Live: https://ricco020.github.io/vpn-black-friday-total-cost/
