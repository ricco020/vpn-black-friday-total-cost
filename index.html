Black Friday VPN deals 2026: the total, not the monthly price

Every Black Friday VPN plan is advertised per month, but each one covers a different number of months, is billed upfront and in a different currency. This table puts the plans side by side by what leaves your account over the period you choose. When a provider does not print its renewal price, the tool says so instead of inventing one.

Euro and dollar totals are not converted into each other: the currency is the one shown on the provider's page when it was read. VAT may be added at checkout depending on your country.

How the total is counted

The first term is billed once, on day one. If the period you pick is longer than the first term, the plan renews for 12 months at its renewal price, billed upfront again. A plan whose renewal price was not printed on the order page shows "renewal not published" for any period past its first term, because the real figure is unknown. Turning auto-renewal off before the first term ends avoids the renewal charge.

Prices come from the table in VPNSmith's Black Friday and Cyber Monday VPN deals guide, read on each provider's own order page on the date shown in the last column (NordVPN on 14 November 2026, the others on 16 November 2026). Black Friday prices change during the sale and by country, so check the order page before you buy.
